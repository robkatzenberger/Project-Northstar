# Ten-agent Switchboard stress test — completed

**Result:** all 10,000 planned pings reconciled across independently written gate and agent logs. 4,700 returned PASS and 5,300 returned DENY, exactly as their round's whitelist required. No incorrect outcomes, missing or duplicate ping records, identity mismatches, IPC send errors, or response timeouts were found. All ten workers exited successfully.

**Run:** `stress-20260910T042441767Z-25990a2a`, 2026-09-10T04:24:41.767Z through 2026-09-10T04:24:57.444Z (UTC; September 9 in America/Chicago). This is builder evidence from one local run, not independent security acceptance.

## Ten increasingly fast rounds

Ten concurrent **Node.js test-agent processes** each sent 100 pings per round. They are process agents, not ten AI-model agents. The gate was one parent process calling the unchanged synchronous Switchboard. Registry snapshots served as the test whitelist database; no production database was introduced or modified.

Every round selected a fresh uniform random count from 0 through 10 and an unbiased shuffled subset of the ten enrolled identities. The manifest saved all draws before the first ping. Both zero-whitelisted and all-whitelisted cases occurred naturally. No rerolls were used.

All ten rounds actually completed faster than their predecessor. The final 1,000-ping burst completed in **12.80 ms**. Its short-window completion rate is a measurement of this burst, not a sustained throughput or capacity claim. Whitelist mix and runtime warm-up also varied across rounds.

The burst raised p99 round-trip latency from **0.42 ms** in round 9 to **10.53 ms** in round 10. Each worker reached 100 outstanding pings. This shows the additional waiting under a burst; it did not produce an incorrect screening decision or missing response. Node IPC reported zero send-backpressure signals throughout this run, which does not mean no queuing occurred. Burst agent send spans ranged from 1.08 to 2.03 ms rather than literally zero time.

The local round-trip measurement includes IPC, scheduling, queuing, and evidence-writing overhead. Gate-only evaluation percentiles are retained separately in the [verification output](stress-20260910T042441767Z-25990a2a/verification.json). Each duration is measured within one process using its own monotonic clock.

## Claim boundary

The tested Switchboard held its whitelist boundary under the recorded cooperative local load. PASS means only **proceed to constraint screening**. This run did not test full constraint evaluation, human verification, execution authorization, hostile agents/process isolation, a network service, live revocation during a round, or a production database. Registry changes were intentionally applied at drained round boundaries. Random selection covered six distinct whitelist sizes in this run, not every possible subset. Neither these records nor the checker establish tamper resistance against a shared-host attacker.
